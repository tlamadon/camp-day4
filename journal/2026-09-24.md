## Later: a fourth estimator

**Goal**  Add to `SPEC.md`, implement, and run an estimator that fits the *whole*
          variance–covariance matrix of growth by GMM, recovering ρ and the other
          variance parameters rather than one moment ratio.

**Did**   `Ω_ts = 2σ²_ε/(1+ρ)` on the diagonal, `−σ²_ε(1−ρ)/(1+ρ)·ρ^(|t−s|−1)` off
          it — the matrix already derived and machine-checked in
          `proofs/PanelAR1/GrowthACov.lean`. Minimum distance in Frobenius norm
          between it and `C = ΔY′ΔY/N`, which is GMM on all T² growth moments
          with an identity weight.
          - Ω is linear in σ²_ε, so σ²_ε concentrates out and the criterion
            profiles down to one parameter on (−1, 1): a 1,001-point grid then
            golden section, no optimiser dependency.
          - The efficient weight is not available — the moment vector repeats
            each pair, and at T = 50 there are 1,275 distinct moments against
            N = 500 draws — but a fixed weight never inverts Ŝ, only sandwiches
            it, so the clustered SEs are still exact. Uninformative moments
            down-weight themselves: ∂Ω_ts/∂θ decays like ρ^|t−s|.
          - Never builds the N × T² matrix of per-individual moments: the
            sandwich needs two N-vectors, `Δy_i′ (∂Ω/∂θ_p) Δy_i`, one matmul each.
          - Results: ρ̂ within 0.023 of 0.700 and σ̂_ε within 0.003 of 0.300 in
            **both** models at every T, coverage 0.98 and 0.96, SE-to-dispersion
            1.14 and 0.96. The five biased cells are unmoved. Pilot grid 3.3 s,
            R = 1,000 grid 19.1 s — two to three times the three-estimator cost.
          - Deliverable 5, the σ_ε table, in markdown, LaTeX and the HTML page;
            `summary.csv` carries the same five statistics for σ̂_ε.
          - 88 tests. The new ones check Ω against the simulated growth at
            N = 500,000, that feeding the population Ω back returns its own
            parameters, and that shifting every individual by a constant leaves
            the estimate untouched — the invariance that makes it work in M1.

**Failed** The palette's fourth categorical slot is yellow, which sits next to
          orange; that pair fails the *all-pairs* floors, and both figures put
          four series in one frame. The adjacent pairlist — the one the palette
          assigns to lines — passes in both modes, so the slot stands, with
          marker shape and direct labels carrying identity as well as colour.
          Two label collisions the old code could not have had: in M1 the within
          estimator ends at 0.664 and the GMM at 0.700, and in M0 pooled OLS and
          the GMM are both consistent and land on top of each other. Both figures
          now place labels by a rule instead of a threshold.
          Tolerances: the GMM criterion is flat at its maximum, so 1e-16 in `C`
          becomes ~1e-8 in ρ̂. The first invariance test asserted `rel=1e-8` and
          failed on the truth.

**Open**  σ_α is not identified from growth — that absence is exactly why the
          estimator survives M1 — so recovering it needs the levels moment
          Var(y_it) added to the growth moments. Written up as an extension, not
          built.
          The Lean proof directory and its `mise.toml` entry are still
          uncommitted work; `SPEC.md` now cites the file.

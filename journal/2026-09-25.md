## Later: M2, and a fifth estimator

**Goal**  Add a DGP where the persistence is latent and what is recorded is
          measured with error — `p_it = ρ p_i,t−1 + ε_it`, `y_it = α_i + p_it +
          ν_it` — re-derive Ω for it, and add the estimator those moments imply.

**Did**   Δy = Δp + Δν with the two independent, so Ω is a sum: the AR(1) growth
          matrix plus the MA(1) that differencing white noise leaves.

              Ω = σ²_ε A(ρ) + σ²_ν B,   B = 2 on the diagonal, −1 next to it, 0 beyond.

          The shapes are told apart by reach — A's off-diagonals decay forever,
          B's stop after one — so ρ is pinned by Ω_{k+1}/Ω_k for k ≥ 2 at T ≥ 4,
          and by Ω_2/(Ω_0 + 2Ω_1) = (ρ−1)/2 at T = 3. T = 2 is one equation short.
          - Still linear in the variances, so both concentrate out as a 2 × 2
            least squares per ρ and only ρ is searched. Variances are held
            non-negative; with two coefficients the NNLS answer is one of four
            candidates, so all four are evaluated.
          - New `growth.py` holds Ω, both shapes and the criterion. The
            estimators wrap it around a sample C, and `analytics.py` feeds it a
            *population* Ω to get plims — which is how the pseudo-true values
            below are exact rather than guessed, and cannot drift from the code
            that produces the estimates.
          - Every plim is now model-dependent, so the closed forms of M0/M1 were
            generalised to run off (σ²_ℓ, γ): pooled, FD, and a trace-ratio for
            within that is exact at finite T. A test pins the general within
            formula to Nickell at every T, which is what caught that the rest of
            the generalisation was right.
          - **The result.** Only the ME fit is consistent in all three models.
            The AR(1) growth GMM — the reference in M0 and M1 — collapses to
            0.370 in M2, worse than the within estimator at T = 50: with no σ_ν
            to hold the noise it loads it onto σ_ε (0.354) and pays for the shape
            mismatch by lowering ρ. FD doubles to −0.301, within picks up
            attenuation on top of Nickell and tends to 0.571 rather than ρ.
          - The price: in M0 and M1 the ME fit is consistent but biased in short
            panels — +0.045 at T = 3, N = 500, falling to 0.014 at N = 5,000 and
            0.005 at N = 50,000 — from the non-negativity constraint binding
            about half the time and T = 3 leaving it exactly identified. Checked
            rather than assumed, because at R = 10 it is the largest gap in the
            grid and would otherwise read as a bug.
          - 122 tests, 75 cells, five slots of the palette, three panels per figure.
          - The grid has two definitions now, and only one of them was mine:
            `.hut/workflows/hpc-grid.json`, pushed from the other end of the
            session, enumerated one task per cell and so still said 2 models.
            Extended to 15 from `config.MODELS` and `config.T_GRID` rather than
            by hand, with the M2 tasks copied off the M1 template so the two
            cannot drift; its run summary and outputs now name the variance
            table too.
